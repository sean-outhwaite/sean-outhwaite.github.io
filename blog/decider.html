Decider

Dipping my toes in React Native

24 Apr 2026

What should we watch?

When it comes time for movie/tv night, my partner and I often spend ages trying to decide what to watch. I wanted to create an app where we could both submit suggestions as we come across them, then the other person can approve or decline. Once both of us have approved something, it goes into our watchlist so that we can just pick something off the list instead of browsing Netflix for 20 minutes.

The Challenge

I've never worked with React Native before, so following the advice in their official docs I decided to use Expo to get started. This really helped to shortcut some of the project setup, and once I got started I found it pretty easy to build out with my existing React knowledge. I think the hardest part was figuring out how to build the app and get it loaded on physical devices without publishing to app stores (Apple make this particularly difficult).

One other issue that I haven't quite been able to solve, is a visual problem with 2 buttons side by side. I think it's just a contrast issue but in some cases the red button to reject a suggestion can look like it's larger or over top of the green approval button - it isn't, but it does look a little weird.

Databased

I initially wrote the app using a local SQLite DB, which worked well for testing and getting logic setup but wasn't going to cut it when I wanted multiple people to be able to work with the same data. After looking at a few options, I ended up rolling with Firestore to handle the data. It's a simple to manage solution and also meant that I could use Firebase to handle the auth side of things.

Featuring

As mentioned, Decider lets my partner and I submit suggestions and then approve each others. It has a light/dark mode, swipe to delete, and some lovely navigation/modals. Because I don't plan on publishing it, I made user management super simple - it will detect what platform it's running on and sign in as my partner if it's Android or myself if it's iOS. In the future it should be easy to convert this to a traditional sign in if I decide to open this up to more users.
